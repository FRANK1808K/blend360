# Flashcards (209 Anki cards)

🌐 [Español](README.md) · **English**

[`blend360-flashcards.csv`](blend360-flashcards.csv) contains 209 cards with 3 columns: `front`, `back` and `tags`. The front is the English term or concept and the back is the Spanish explanation, so every card trains your English and the technical content at the same time.

## Importing into Anki (free: [apps.ankiweb.net](https://apps.ankiweb.net/); on Android, AnkiDroid)

1. Create a deck called **Blend360**.
2. **File → Import** and choose `blend360-flashcards.csv`.
3. Settings: *Field separator*: **Comma** · ✅ *Allow HTML* · *First row is header*.
4. Map the fields: column 1 → **Front**, column 2 → **Back**, column 3 → **Tags**.
5. Import. Study **20 new cards a day** + the reviews Anki asks for.

> 💡 **Train both directions:** choose the note type **"Basic (and reversed card)"** when importing. Anki will then also ask you Spanish → English, which is what you need to *speak* in the interview.

💡 Anki uses **spaced repetition**: it shows you each card just before you would forget it. With 15–20 minutes **every day**, you will know all 209 by December 18.

**Add your own cards** every time you find a word or concept you did not know. The best cards are the ones you write yourself.

No Anki? Import the CSV into [Quizlet](https://quizlet.com/) (Create → Import, separator: comma) or just open it in Excel / Google Sheets.

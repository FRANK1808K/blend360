# 🇬🇧 English for the Blend360 exam: from A2 to "functional" in 8 weeks

> **Your situation:** A2 level, a technical exam in English on **December 18**. In 8 weeks you will not reach C1, and you do not need to. What you need is:
>
> 1. **Read and understand technical questions** without freezing. This is the **most important** skill for the written test.
> 2. **Recognize the "trap" words** in questions (NOT, EXCEPT, LEAST, at least…).
> 3. **Introduce yourself and explain your solution** with simple sentences, in case there is an interview (Blend360 usually has technical and HR interviews after the test, and it works with US clients).
>
> Technical vocabulary (SQL, Python, AWS) is very similar to Spanish and you already use it every day. **That is your starting point.**

## Daily routine (30–40 min), **every day** until December 18

| Block | Min | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Flashcards | 10 | Anki with [`flashcards/blend360-flashcards.csv`](../flashcards/README.en.md). Spaced repetition is the most efficient way to memorize |
| 2. Technical reading | 10 | Read **in English** the statements of the SQL/Python exercises in this repo, or a LeetCode/HackerRank problem. Underline unknown words and add them to Anki |
| 3. Listening | 10 | A short technical video **in English with English subtitles** (see resources below). Use 0.75× speed if needed |
| 4. Speaking | 5–10 | Read your self-introduction **out loud** or explain in English the exercise you solved today (record yourself on your phone) |

> 💡 **High-impact trick:** switch the language of your phone, VS Code and browser to English **today**. And from now on, **always read the practice statements in English first**.

## Rules to make a mock exam useful

1. **Real conditions:** a timer, your phone away, no Internet searches.
2. **Finish first, then check.** Do not look at the answers halfway.
3. **Record your result** in the table below (edit it in your copy of the repo).
4. For every mistake, write down **why** you failed: I did not know the concept, I misread the English, I ran out of time, or it was carelessness. Attack the most frequent cause.

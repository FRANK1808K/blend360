# What does Blend360 evaluate? (research)

[Español](que-evalua-blend360.md) · **English**

> A summary of what is publicly known about the process, researched on **October 26, 2026**. The sources are candidate reviews and third-party guides, **not** official Blend360 information, and the format may change. Always confirm with your recruiter (see [questions for the recruiter](#5-questions-worth-asking-the-recruiter)).

---

## 1. The company and the program

- **Blend360 (Blend)** is a **data science, analytics and AI** company that works as a consultancy for international clients, mainly in the United States.
- **In Colombia:** it acquired the Colombian AI company **Nuvu** in 2025 and presents Colombia as an innovation hub for Latin America. It has reportedly planned to grow to **1,000 people** in the country.
- **All Star Program:** a program for people at the start of their careers (recent graduates in quantitative fields: data science, analytics, statistics, computer/systems engineering). Job postings describe it as a **consultant training program of about 6 months**, with **Data Engineering, Data Science and Business Intelligence** tracks.

## 2. The AWS relationship (why AWS is in your syllabus)

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Partner tier | **AWS Premier Tier Services Partner** (according to Blend's page; it previously reached the Advanced tier) |
| AWS competencies | **Data & Analytics**, **Generative AI** (earned in 2025), Financial Services, DevOps, Migration |
| Service designations | **Amazon EMR Delivery Partner** and **Amazon QuickSight Delivery Partner** |
| Team | More than 300 AWS-certified experts (according to its page) |
| Awards | AWS awards at re:Invent 2025 (for example, Sustainability Partner of the Year, LATAM) |

**Takeaway for your study:** master the **AWS data ecosystem** (S3, Glue, Athena, Redshift, **EMR**, Kinesis, **QuickSight**, Lake Formation) and cloud fundamentals. It is all in [06-aws](../06-aws/README.en.md). Your **AWS AI Practitioner** certification (Nov 16) is a plus: mention it in the interview.

## 3. What candidates report about the process

| Stage | What was reported | Source |
|---|---|---|
| HR screening | Initial call or video | Several reviews |
| **Logic and abstract reasoning tests** | In **Bogotá (May 2026)**, a candidate describes them as **"quite demanding"**, after the HR screening | Glassdoor |
| **Online technical assessment** | About **2 hours**, with **2 Python (or R) problems and 2 SQL problems** (data science profile) | Interview Query |
| SQL under pressure | A BI Analyst candidate reports **hard** SQL, about **12 minutes per question**, 5 questions, "it doesn't let you make mistakes" | Glassdoor |
| Platform | One candidate mentions a **SQL challenge on Coderbyte** | Glassdoor |
| Technical interviews | JOINs, **window functions**, statistics/ML (depending on the profile) and BI tool tests (Tableau, Power BI) | Glassdoor, Interview Query |
| Behavioral interviews | "Tell me about yourself and your most impactful project", teamwork, disagreements with colleagues, how you optimized your code | Glassdoor, Scoutify |
| Presentation | Some reviews mention presenting a project or a case | Glassdoor, Interview Query |
| Process length | Between 7–10 days and 4–6 weeks, depending on the role | Glassdoor, Dataford |

## 4. What this means for your preparation

1. **SQL is the heaviest topic** and can be hard → master **window functions, CTEs, JOINs and aggregations**, and practice **against the clock** (12 min per problem). See [02-sql](../02-sql/README.en.md).
2. **Data-oriented Python** (handling lists, dictionaries, strings, pandas) + basic algorithms → [03-python](../03-python/README.en.md) and [04-algorithms](../04-algoritmos-y-estructuras/README.en.md).
3. **Abstract reasoning is demanding** → daily practice, with a timer. See [01-abstract reasoning](../01-razonamiento-abstracto/README.en.md).
4. **English:** the test is in English and Blend works with US clients → reading exam questions + your self-introduction. See [english](../english/README.en.md).
5. **Statistics and ML** (Data Science tracks): A/B tests, p-values, model metrics, overfitting → [08-statistics and ML](../08-estadistica-y-ml/README.en.md).
6. **Behavioral:** prepare 2–3 **STAR** stories about projects, teamwork and a challenge.
7. **Platform:** if they tell you it is Coderbyte, HackerRank or similar, do 3–4 practice challenges **on that platform** to learn its editor and test cases.

## 5. Questions worth asking the recruiter

It is completely normal to ask this before a test (in Spanish or in English):

- Which **platform** is used for the test (Coderbyte, HackerRank, TestGorilla, another one)?
- Which **SQL engine** does the test use (PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server)? Can I choose between Python and R?
- Is the technical test **multiple choice, coding, or both**?
- Can I use **scratch paper**? Is there a camera / **proctoring**?
- Do wrong answers **subtract points**?
- Is there a separate **English** assessment or an interview in English?
- What are the next steps after the test?

*Ready to send:* "Could you tell me which platform the technical test uses, and which SQL dialect? Is it multiple choice, coding, or both?"

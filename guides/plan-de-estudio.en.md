# Study plan: October 26 → December 18, 2026

[Español](plan-de-estudio.md) · **English**

> **Two exams, one plan.**
>
> - **November 16 (Monday):** AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01).
> - **December 18 (Friday):** Blend360 All Star test (abstract reasoning, 30 min + technical test, 90 min, in English).
>
> Until November 16 the priority is **AWS**, but **every day** you keep a base for Blend360 (English + reasoning + SQL). What you study for AWS also counts for Blend360: Blend is an **AWS Premier Tier Partner** and the test covers "what AWS is and data analytics on AWS".

## PHASE 1: AWS AIF-C01 + Blend360 base

For AWS, use the integrated module **[AWS AI Practitioner in Spanish](../aws-ai-practitioner/README.en.md)** ([domains](../aws-ai-practitioner/README.en.md#domains), [mock exams](../aws-ai-practitioner/practice-questions/README.md) and [retake plan](../aws-ai-practitioner/guides/retake-plan.md)).

### Week 1 · Oct 26 – Nov 1

- [ ] **AWS (2 h/day):** AIF-C01 domains 1 and 2 (fundamentals of AI/ML and of generative AI).
- [ ] **Blend (30 min/day):** [SQL README](../02-sql/README.en.md) sections 1–6 + SQL exercises 1–10.
- [ ] Reasoning: series A and B of the [exercises](../01-razonamiento-abstracto/ejercicios.en.md) **without a timer**.
- [ ] English: [how to read exam questions](../english/como-leer-preguntas.en.md) + import the flashcards into Anki.

### Week 2 · Nov 2 – 8

- [ ] **AWS:** domains 3 and 4 (applications of foundation models, responsible AI).
- [ ] **Blend:** [06-aws/README](../06-aws/README.en.md) (cloud fundamentals: useful for both exams) + SQL 11–21.
- [ ] Reasoning: sections C–F of the exercises + simulator in practice mode.
- [ ] English: vocabulary for [AWS and cloud](../english/vocabulario-tecnico.en.md#aws-and-cloud) + [grammar](../english/gramatica-minima.en.md) sections 1–3.

### Week 3 · Nov 9 – 15

- [ ] **AWS:** domain 5 (security and governance) + **2 full AIF-C01 mock exams** + review your mistakes.
- [ ] **Blend (light):** [data analytics on AWS](../06-aws/analisis-de-datos.en.md) + [AWS questions](../06-aws/preguntas.en.md).
- [ ] Reasoning: [reasoning mock exam #1](../simulacros/simulacro-razonamiento-01.en.md) (30 min) → write down your score.
- [ ] Nov 14–15: final AIF-C01 review (no new topics).

### Monday, Nov 16: AWS AIF-C01 exam

Do not study anything for Blend360 that day. Rest after the exam.

## PHASE 2: Blend360 in depth

### Week 4 · Nov 17 – 22: SQL + Python basics

- [ ] Nov 17: active rest (only flashcards and 1 reasoning series).
- [ ] SQL: exercises 22–35 (CASE and window functions) + redo the ones you got wrong.
- [ ] Python: the whole [03-python README](../03-python/README.en.md) + the "what is the output?" quiz.
- [ ] Python: [ejercicios.py](../03-python/ejercicios.py) 1–13 (`python3 test_ejercicios.py`).
- [ ] *Optional:* 5 problems from [LeetCode SQL 50](https://leetcode.com/studyplan/top-sql-50/).
- [ ] English: SQL and Python [vocabulary](../english/vocabulario-tecnico.en.md).

### Week 5 · Nov 23 – 29: advanced Python + algorithms

- [ ] Python: exercises 14–25 + [pandas](../03-python/pandas.en.md) + run `pandas_practica.py`.
- [ ] Algorithms: the whole [04-algorithms README](../04-algoritmos-y-estructuras/README.en.md) (Big O, data structures, sorting, pseudocode tracing).
- [ ] Algorithms: [problemas.py](../04-algoritmos-y-estructuras/problemas.py) (the first 11).
- [ ] English: write and memorize your **self-introduction** ([interview and phrases](../english/entrevista-y-frases.en.md)).
- [ ] Reasoning: simulator, 1 full 30-minute mock exam.

### Week 6 · Nov 30 – Dec 6: algorithms + databases + problem solving + statistics/ML

- [ ] Algorithms: the intermediate problems (the remaining 9).
- [ ] The whole [05-databases](../05-bases-de-datos/README.en.md) module.
- [ ] The whole [07-problem solving](../07-resolucion-de-problemas/README.en.md) module (includes logic, statistics and business cases).
- [ ] [08-statistics and ML](../08-estadistica-y-ml/README.en.md): sections 2–6 and 9 + the priority 1 rows of the route through your Drive notes + [25 questions](../08-estadistica-y-ml/preguntas.en.md). If you are placed on the Data Science track, give it priority.
- [ ] **Saturday, Dec 5:** [technical mock exam #1](../simulacros/simulacro-tecnico-01.en.md) (90 min, real conditions).
- [ ] Sunday, Dec 6: analyze your mistakes in the mock exam and review those topics.
- [ ] English: [English exercises](../english/ejercicios-ingles.en.md) + record yourself answering 3 interview questions.

### Week 7 · Dec 7 – 13: mock exams and weak points

- [ ] 3 technical mock exams in the [simulator](../simulator/README.en.md) (40 questions, 50 min) on alternate days.
- [ ] 3 abstract reasoning mock exams (30 min) in the simulator + the [Mensa Norway test](https://test.mensa.no/).
- [ ] Redo SQL exercises 16, 21, 26, 28, 32, 34 and 35 **without looking**.
- [ ] Timed coding problems: 2 SQL (12 min each) + 2 Python (15 min each) per day. Use [HackerRank](https://www.hackerrank.com/) or [Coderbyte](https://coderbyte.com/) to get used to an assessment platform.
- [ ] English: practice your interview answers out loud.

### Week 8 · Dec 14 – 15: wrap-up

- [ ] Last full mock exam: reasoning (30 min) + technical (90 min) **back to back**, like the real day.
- [ ] Review your mistakes. Read the 4 [cheat sheets](../cheatsheets/README.en.md).
